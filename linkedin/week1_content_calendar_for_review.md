**Title:** Why Direct Owner Sale Matters in Cambodia Real Estate

**Draft:**

In real estate transactions, clarity matters.

For international buyers evaluating land or completed property in Cambodia, one of the most important questions is simple: who am I speaking with?

A Direct Owner Sale gives buyers a cleaner path to discussion. The buyer can speak directly with the party that holds the asset, review documents at the source, arrange site inspections, and understand the background of the property without unnecessary layers.

This does not replace due diligence. It makes due diligence more direct.

For developers, investors, and funds looking at Cambodia, I believe the strongest transactions begin with transparent ownership, clear documentation, and face-to-face discussion on the actual site.

That is the approach we are taking as we review selected company-held land and completed property assets for direct sale.

## Post 4

**Type:** Industry Insight  
**Suggested time:** Thursday, 4:00 PM Cambodia / 5:00 PM Singapore  
**Title:** What Buyers Should Verify Before Acquiring Land in Cambodia

**Draft:**

Before acquiring land in Cambodia, buyers should slow down and verify the basics.

A practical due diligence checklist should include:

1. Title and ownership documents
2. Land size and boundary references
3. Current land use and planning context
4. Road access and utilities
5. Site inspection findings
6. Transaction structure and payment terms
7. Any restrictions, encumbrances, or conditions that may affect development

A good opportunity should be able to withstand careful review.

As a developer and builder, I prefer direct conversations with buyers who ask detailed questions. The strongest transactions are not rushed; they are documented, inspected, and understood by both sides.

**Image suggestion:** Buyer due diligence checklist, document review table, site visit photo, or clean professional infographic.
